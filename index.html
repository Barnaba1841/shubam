How to Play

Simple enough to learn quickly. Strategic enough to keep every round interesting.

01

🃏 Get Ready

Shuffle the 120 cards. Deal 7 cards to each player. Place the rest face-down as the Draw Pile.

02

🎬 Start

Place one simple 1–9 card face-up as the first Discard Pile card. The game always starts clockwise.

03

🎨 Match

Play a card that matches the current Era/colour OR the current number. Power Cards follow their own rules.

04

⚡ Use Powers

Use Double Dhamaka, CUT, Interval Bang, Blockbuster Bomma, ATIH and AADHI at the right moment.

05

🎤 Say SHUBAM

When you have exactly 1 card, say shubam! If you forget, draw 3 cards.

06

🏆 Finish

The first player to reach 0 cards finishes 1st. Continue until only one player remains.

⚠️ Draw Rule If you cannot play, draw 1 card. Your turn ends after drawing. You do not keep drawing until you can play.

How to Write Your Cards

The exact Shubam writing system will live here. Once finalized, these rules are treated as fixed game rules.

01Check the Era. Use the official shubam Era/actor classification.
02Check the number. Number cards 1–9 require a movie title that follows the official shubam letter-counting rule.
03Write the title exactly. shubam's official spelling, spacing and punctuation rules are the source of truth.
04One movie per card. The physical 113-card deck uses 113 different movie names.
🔒

Fixed Rules

Once the official writing format is finalized, the website will display it exactly as provided. No automatic reinterpretation.
