01

How to Play

Simple enough to learn quickly. Strategic enough to keep every round interesting.

01

🃏 Get Ready

Shuffle the 120 cards. Deal 7 cards to each player. Place the rest face-down as the Draw Pile.

02

🎬 Start

Place one card face-up as the first Discard Pile card.
The game always starts clockwise.

03

🎨 Match

Play a card that matches the current Era OR the current lettered (1-9).
Power Cards follow their own rules.

04

⚡ Use Powers

Use Double Dhamaka, CUT, Interval Bang, Blockbuster Bomma, All Time Industry Hittu and AADHI at the right moment.

05

🚫 Strucked at

If you don't have any playable cards, draw 1 card. Your turn ends after drawing.

06

🎤 Say SHUBAM

When you have exactly 1 card, say Shubam!
If you forget, draw 3 cards.

🏆 Finishing Points

The first player to reach 0 cards finishes 1st. Continue until only one player remains.
🥇 1st 10 🥈 2nd 9 🥉 3rd 8 4th 7 5th 6 6th 5 7th 4 8th 3 9th 2 10th 0

For games with fewer players, the last player gets 0 points.

03

How to Write Your Cards

The exact SHUBAM writing system will live here. Once finalized, these rules are treated as fixed game rules.

01 Check the Era. Use the official SHUBAM Era/actor classification.
02 Check the number. Number cards 1–9 require a movie title that follows the official SHUBAM letter-counting rule.
03 Write the title exactly. SHUBAM's official spelling, spacing and punctuation rules are the source of truth.
04 One movie per card. The physical 113-card deck uses 113 different movie names.
🔒

Fixed Rules

Once the official writing format is finalized, the website will display it exactly as provided. No automatic reinterpretation.
